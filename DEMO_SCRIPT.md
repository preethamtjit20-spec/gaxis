# G-Axis Demo Script — Gemini Live Agent Challenge

**Total time: 3-4 minutes**

---

## SCENE 1: THE HOOK (0:00 - 0:15)

**[Screen: Clean browser with G-Axis extension icon visible]**

> "Your browser already works. You don't need to download a new browser just to experience AI.
> Meet G-Axis — a Chrome extension that turns your browser into an AI-powered workspace."

---

## SCENE 2: WHAT IT IS (0:15 - 0:30)

**[Screen: Click G-Axis icon, sidepanel opens with the welcome screen]**

> "G-Axis lives inside your browser as a sidepanel. It sees what you see. It talks like a friend. And when you need it to — it acts."

> "No new apps. No login walls. No captcha headaches. Your browser, your logins, your setup — G-Axis just adds the intelligence."

---

## SCENE 3: VOICE COMPANION — LIVE (0:30 - 1:30)

**[Click mic button. Mic popup appears. Orb starts animating.]**

> "Let's talk to it. G-Axis connects directly to Gemini's Live API — real-time, bidirectional voice. Not text-to-speech. Actual conversation."

**[Select "Friendly Buddy" persona]**

**YOU:** "Hey G-Axis, what's happening with AI this week? Anything exciting?"

**[G-Axis responds naturally with voice — searches web via Google Search, gives current info. Transcript flows in real-time.]**

> "It just searched the web live and gave me a real answer. No stale training data."

**[Switch persona to "Job Interviewer"]**

> "G-Axis comes with 8 AI personas — each with its own voice, personality, and purpose."

**YOU:** "Give me a tough interview question for a senior engineer."

**[G-Axis responds in a different, professional voice]**

> "Different voice. Different energy. Same seamless session. Switch personas anytime — mid-conversation."

**[Switch to "Creative Partner"]**

**YOU:** "I need a tagline for an AI browser extension."

**[G-Axis brainstorms energetically]**

> "Friendly Buddy for hanging out. Job Interviewer for practice. Creative Partner for brainstorming. Wise Mentor for advice. Chill Companion for vibes. And three more."

---

## SCENE 4: CONVERSATION INTELLIGENCE (1:30 - 2:00)

**[End voice session. Click "My Progress"]**

> "Every conversation is analyzed by Gemini. Communication skills scored. XP earned. Streaks tracked."

**[Show dashboard: Level card, skill bars (Confidence, Clarity, Engagement), weekly activity dots, recent conversations with AI-generated summaries]**

> "It's not just a chatbot — it's a communication coach that learns how you talk and helps you improve."

---

## SCENE 5: BROWSER AUTOMATION (2:00 - 2:45)

**[Back to welcome. Type: "Plan a 5-day Japan itinerary"]**

> "But G-Axis doesn't just talk. It operates."

**[Activity timeline shows: Task started → Navigator → Researcher scanning sources → Document generated]**

> "Give it a goal. It plans, navigates, researches across multiple sources, and delivers a complete document. Autonomously."

**[.docx opens]**

> "A full Japan itinerary. Researched, compiled, formatted — in under two minutes."

**[Type: "Schedule a meeting for tomorrow at 10am"]**

> "It works with Google Workspace too. Calendar, Gmail, Docs — it fills forms, clicks buttons, understands UI through Gemini Vision."

**[Show Calendar being filled]**

> "And it always asks before doing anything irreversible. You keep control. G-Axis adds the intelligence."

---

**[Quick overlay or voiceover with key points]**

> "How it works:"
> - "Gemini Live API — direct WebSocket, zero-latency voice"
> - "Gemini Vision — sees and understands any webpage"
> - "Google Search — real-time web data in conversation"
> - "8 personas, 5 voices, live switching"
> - "Communication analytics with skill scoring"
> - "7 specialist agents, 34 connector skills"
> - "Deployed on Google Cloud Run with Terraform"
> - "OAuth tokens — no API keys exposed, ever"

---

## SCENE 7: THE CLOSE (3:15 - 3:30)

**[Screen: G-Axis sidepanel with orb, dashboard, and welcome screen]**

> "Your browser extends G-Axis' reach. Your logins. Your setup. Your habits."

> "G-Axis extends your browser's intelligence."

> "Stop switching tabs. Start delegating."

> "G-Axis. Built for the Gemini Live Agent Challenge."

## BACKUP PLAN

If voice fails mid-demo:
> "Voice runs on Gemini's Live API — sometimes the connection needs a moment. Let me show the browser automation while it reconnects."

Then switch to typing a task. Voice usually reconnects within seconds.
